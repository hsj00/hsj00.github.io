Q1. 비즈니스 이메일 작성 — 5.5 / 10
원문 (Original)
"As we discussed yesterday, we decided to adapt new etching process next quarter. I believe every guys already know the details. Please send me an email if you have any questions. Thanks."
첨삭본 (Corrected)
"As we discussed yesterday, we have decided to adopt a new etching process starting next quarter. Please refer to the meeting notes for further details. Feel free to reach out if you have any questions.
Best regards,"

Q2. 미팅 중 반박 발언 — 6 / 10
원문 (Original)
"You claimed that the reason of low yield rate was due to equipment. I know what you mean but I think that the process parameters can cause this issue, too. We already checked the process parameter data based on wafer level test. Please consider that the process parameters are the reason seriously."
첨삭본 (Corrected)
"I understand your point about the equipment issue. However, based on our wafer-level test data, I'd also like to suggest that process parameters could be a contributing factor. The data we reviewed shows some correlation with parameter variations. I'd strongly recommend we investigate both possibilities before drawing a conclusion."

표현 약함I know what you mean butI see your point, however...더 프로페셔널한 전환 표현
